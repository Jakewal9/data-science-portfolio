What is Data Science to Me?

Learning data science in a social and interdisciplinary context is important to ensure that the models are ethical and have sound research design.
Modeling social topics and people helps us understand and predict human behaviors and structures in society.

Responsibilities that come with using data about people are to make sure it is being obtained and used ethically and legally.

I am hoping to better understand how to analyze data to explore social challenges and the impact they have on the world. Learning how to ethically collect and use data from external sources and the best way to find data.
I am also looking forward to sharpening my coding skills and better understand what exactly the code is saying.
